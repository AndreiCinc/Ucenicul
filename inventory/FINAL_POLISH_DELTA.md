## What was changed (minimum-viable patch)

In `inventory/final_consistency_audit.json` and `inventory/final_consistency_audit_post.json`:

- **Removed** `status_before_fix` (it was a redundant pre-state placeholder, not history of a real intermediate state).
- **Added** `status` — computed from results: `"PASSED — SYNCHRONIZED"` (since `mismatches_found == 0` and `summary.failed == 0`); would be `"REGENERATION_REQUIRED"` otherwise.
- **Added** `status_history` — preserves the original pre-seeded label as `historical_pre_sync_state` and pairs it with `current_state` so the lineage is auditable without contaminating the active state.
- **Added** `polish_log[]` (only in `final_consistency_audit_post.json`, the snapshot artifact) — records this Phase-9b semantic edit with timestamp + reason.

Also **patched** `scripts/final_consistency_audit.py` (and its mirror under `scripts/migration/`) so future runs:

- do not pre-seed the stale placeholder
- compute `status` from results at end-of-run
- emit `status_history` for lineage

This makes the script idempotent: re-running it on the synchronized tree now produces a clean artifact with `status: "PASSED — SYNCHRONIZED"` and no stale placeholders.
